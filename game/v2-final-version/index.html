🍞 HOW TO PLAY 🍞

Goal: Be the first to reach 50 points!

ROLL: Roll two dice to add to your Current Heat.

END TURN: Bank your Current Heat into your Total Score.

🎲 THE DICE RULES 🎲

Any 1: You lose all your Current Heat and your turn ends automatically.

Snake Eyes (1+1): Your TOTAL score is wiped to zero. Ouch.

Anything else: Points added to your Current Heat — keep rolling or bank it!

⚠️ THE FINE PRINT ⚠️

First to 50+ points wins. Don't get greedy!
